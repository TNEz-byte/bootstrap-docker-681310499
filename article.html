Overview of Sports Cars

A sports car is a automobile designed with an emphasis on dynamic performance, handling, aerodynamics, and driving thrill. Unlike standard passenger vehicles that prioritize utility and cargo capacity, sports cars are built to deliver maximum engagement between the driver and the road.
